Option A — Via Google Calendar (easiest)

Sync WorkAxle → Google Calendar first, then add your Google account to Apple Calendar. Shifts appear automatically.

On your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Calendar → Accounts → Add Account
  3. Tap Google and sign in
  4. Make sure Calendars is toggled on
  5. Open the Calendar app — shifts appear within minutes

Option B — Live iCal Feed (always on)

Deploy the included cloudflare-worker.js to Cloudflare Workers (free), then enter your Worker URL below. The extension will automatically keep it updated with fresh tokens.

Paste this URL into Apple Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription. Set refresh to Every Hour.
